Keep the real reported decline and explain time-of-day limitations, while identifying the official Terra/Aqua MODIS plus S-NPP mixture and September–November versus October–November window difference. ## Live snapshot

`scripts/fetch_live.mjs` fetches only the four NASA FIRMS public seven-day CSVs, retries up to five attempts with backoff, validates headers/rows and computes detection counts and median local solar time for each platform and app preset. It splits Terra/Aqua from the combined MODIS feed. All-observation and daytime-only medians are separate. Median times are conditioned on detected activity, not an unbiased orbit schedule.

A feed failure retains that feed's last successful data and original fetch date, marked stale. With no prior snapshot it remains unavailable, not zero. A successful header-only response means no new data returned. NRT values never enter the historical merged calendar.

The workflow emits a snapshot artifact; deployment remains a separate step. Do not describe the static site as continuously live.
